Overview

Founded in 1924, the Irrigation Research Institute (IRI) is one of Punjab's oldest and most respected institutions dedicated to irrigation science, hydraulic engineering, and water resources management. Over the decades, it has grown into a center of excellence known for its technical expertise and contributions to the region's irrigation infrastructure.

Established during the expansion of canal systems in the subcontinent, IRI was created to address emerging agricultural and engineering challenges through scientific research and practical solutions.
